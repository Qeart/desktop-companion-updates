# Pet portraits

207 portraits, keyed by the game's Pet ID, copied without artwork changes from
`data/pets/*.png` in https://github.com/keytftw/aion2pettracker at commit
`8a57ba3c36ce0cccb9c783c35ffba4fd2983c3f6` (GPL-3.0).
All 207 ID/name pairs were checked against Aurum's existing catalog.

The source documents these as 64-pixel PNG conversions of the Questlog game-data
snapshot published by xCROv/aion2-pet-tracker, tree
`93da8f0a4d672210eb455c20bbea7dcc9191cd5b`.
Original catalog and portraits: https://questlog.gg/aion-2/en/db/pets .
